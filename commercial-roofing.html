Commercial FAQs

Straight answers for owners and managers.

How long does a commercial roof last?

Most commercial roofs last somewhere between 15 and 30 years, depending on the system, installation quality and maintenance. Texas sun, heat and storms can shorten that range if the roof isn’t inspected and serviced regularly.

How often should a commercial roof be inspected?

At least once a year, plus after major storms with high wind or hail. Regular inspections catch open seams, punctures and clogged drains before they turn into leaks.

Do I have to shut down during a replacement?

In most cases, no. We plan projects so your building can stay open as much as possible — staging work in sections, coordinating around your busiest hours and keeping your team informed.

Can a coating extend the life of my roof?

In the right situation, yes. If the existing roof is still structurally sound, a coating or restoration system can seal seams and improve weather resistance. We always inspect first and tell you whether your roof is a good candidate or replacement makes more sense.

Do you work with property managers and multiple buildings?

Yes. We evaluate each roof, prioritize repairs and help you plan replacements across a portfolio.
