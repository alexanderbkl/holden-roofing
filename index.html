Good to know

Questions, answered.

Which areas do you serve?

We have local teams in Austin (about a 48-mile range), San Antonio (about 66 miles), Arlington (about 60 miles) and Houston (about 42 miles). See the map above to check your address.

Do you handle emergency and storm damage?

Yes. Storm, wind and emergency hail damage repair are core services, and same-day or next-day bookings are available. Call your local office for the fastest response.

What roof types do you work on?

Composite shingles, metal roofs, clay tiles, slate and wood shingles — plus flat roofs, gutters, chimneys, flashing and skylights.

How do I get a quote?

Call your local team or send the form above. We’ll discuss your roof, the work needed and pricing with you directly.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Robinson & Sons Roofing & Remodeling is licensed, bonded and insured.

What are your hours and how can I pay?

Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM, and Saturday & Sunday 9 AM–3 PM. We accept credit cards.
