How it works

A simple, four-step path to a better roof.

No surprises. You’ll always know what’s happening, who’s doing it and what comes next.

  1. Free roof inspection

    We visit your home or business, inspect the roof and document what we see — including damage that’s hard to spot from the yard.

  2. Plain-language options

    We explain the condition of your roof and walk through repair versus replacement, with straightforward pricing for your specific project.

  3. Choose & schedule

    Pick materials and colors with real samples in hand, then lock in a schedule that works for you. Financing is available.

  4. Installed & cleaned up

    Our crew protects your property, finishes most single-family roofs in about a day, and cleans up before they leave.

Good questions

Answers before you call.

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. Every project starts with a free roof inspection. We look at your roof, document what we find and tell you honestly whether it needs a repair, a replacement or nothing at all.

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends on the size and shape of your home, the materials you choose, how steep the roof is and whether there’s underlying damage. The best way to get a real number is a free inspection and estimate — we break out materials and labor so you can see where your money is going.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family roof replacements are finished in about a day once the crew starts. Larger or more complex roofs can take longer, and we never rush when conditions are unsafe. We walk you through what to expect before we begin.

Do you help after hail or wind storms?

Yes. We inspect for storm damage that’s easy to miss from the ground and provide emergency tarping to help stop active leaks. We document what we find and explain your options in plain language.

Which shingles do you install?

Mostly Owens Corning. We’re a Platinum Preferred Contractor, which means we meet the manufacturer’s higher standards for installation and service and can offer strong warranty options.

What if I have a concern after the roof is finished?

Call our office and we’ll schedule a visit. Standing behind our work is a big part of how we’ve stayed in business since 1961.
