Financing FAQs

Good to know.

Who provides the financing?

Financing is offered through Service Financial. All financing is subject to credit approval.

What are the terms?

The advertised example is 9.99% APR for 120 months. The amount financed ranges from $3,000 to $100,000, and your monthly payment will vary with the amount you finance.

How is “as low as $39 per month” calculated?

It’s the example payment on the minimum $3,000 financed at 9.99% APR over 120 months. Larger projects will have higher payments — use the estimator above to see yours.

Do I have to finance?

No. Financing is just one option. Every project starts with a free inspection and estimate, with no obligation.
