Know the signs

Damage that’s easy to miss from the ground.

Hail bruising

Small impacts that bruise shingles and loosen protective granules.

Wind damage

Missing, lifted or creased shingles and exposed underlayment.

Tile & flashing

Cracked tile, shifted flashing and debris impact around penetrations.

After the storm

What to do, step by step.

  1. Stay safe

    Stay off the roof and away from damaged areas. If water is coming in, move belongings and call us.

  2. Call for a tarp

    We can tarp and cover the damaged area quickly to limit interior damage while a plan is made.

  3. Get a free inspection

    We check the whole roof — including damage you can’t see from the driveway — and document what we find.

  4. Choose your next step

    Repair or replace, on your schedule. We explain your options in plain language, with no pressure.

Storm FAQs

Common questions after a storm.

Is the storm damage inspection free?

Yes. We come out for a free inspection, document what we find and explain your options in plain language.

Can you tarp my roof right away?

We provide emergency tarping and cover-up work for residential and commercial clients to help stop active leaks. Call {{phone}} and we’ll get a crew scheduled.

Will you help with my insurance claim?

Many of our customers come to us after storm damage. During your inspection we document what we find so you have clear information to share with your insurance company. Coverage decisions are always made by your insurer.

My roof looks fine from the ground. Should I still get it checked?

Yes. Hail bruising and a few missing shingles are easy to miss from the yard but can lead to leaks later. A free inspection tells you where you stand.
