Residential FAQs

What homeowners ask us most.

How much does a new residential roof cost?

It depends on your home’s size and shape, the shingles you choose, how steep the roof is and whether there’s underlying damage. Because there are so many variables, the best way to get a real number is a free inspection and estimate for your specific project.

What time of year is cheapest to replace a roof?

There isn’t a time of year that’s consistently cheapest — shingle prices move with raw-material costs. If your roof is failing, replacing it as soon as practical helps prevent leaks and costlier interior repairs.

How long does a shingle roof last in Texas?

Many Texas shingle roofs last roughly 15–25 years depending on materials, installation and weather. Hail, strong wind and intense sun can shorten that, so regular inspections and small repairs help you get more life from your roof.

Do I need a repair or a replacement?

Repeated leaks, widespread shingle damage, sagging areas or heavy granule loss can mean it’s time to replace. A few missing shingles or a minor leak around a vent can often be repaired. We’ll show you what we see and give you an honest recommendation.

Which shingle brand is best?

There are several good brands, but we install a lot of Owens Corning. As a Platinum Preferred Contractor we meet their higher standards for installation and service and can offer strong, manufacturer-backed warranty options.
