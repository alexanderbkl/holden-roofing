About us FAQs

Frequently asked about Holden.

How long has Holden Roofing been in business?

Our family has been roofing homes in Texas since 1961, completing work for many thousands of homeowners across the state.

Is Holden Roofing still family owned?

Yes. Holden Roofing started as a family business and has stayed that way. The company has grown, but the family name and involvement are still at our center.

Who will I work with during my project?

You’ll have a main point of contact on the sales or project management side, a trained crew on site and an office team that can answer questions.

Are your crews experienced and trained?

Yes. Our crews work on roofs every day and handle a wide range of systems and materials. Many team members have been with Holden for years, which keeps workmanship consistent.

How do I tell if a roofer is being straight with me?

A good roofer explains things simply, puts details in writing and answers questions without pressure. Red flags include pushing you to sign on the spot or changing the story between visits. Check reviews, ask how long the company has been in business and make sure there’s a real office and team behind the person at the door.
